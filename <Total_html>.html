Australian Domestic Air Travel (2004–2019) — Interactive Data Dashboard

Data sources: audomcitypairs-201912.csv (monthly passenger traffic) and DomesticAirlines.csv (departures and cancellations). Time period: 2004–2019. This dashboard provides an integrated view of passenger traffic trends and operational reliability across Australia’s major domestic airports.

Map: Cancellation Rate by Departure Airport (2004–2019)

The map visualizes cancellation rates by departure airport. Bubble size encodes the total number of actual departures (flights), while color represents the proportion of cancelled flights over the period. Australia only.

Chart 1: Monthly Passenger Traffic by Route (Filterable)

Explore monthly passenger traffic for a selected route. Use the origin/destination dropdowns and the start-year slider to adjust the time window. The dashed rule and label mark the month with peak passenger volume within the filtered range.

Chart 2: Top-10 Routes by Year (Total Passengers)

View the ten busiest domestic routes for a chosen year. Bars are ranked by total passenger volume. Use the year slider to examine year-to-year variation.
