Australian Domestic Routes — Interactive Visualisations

Data: audomcitypairs-201912.csv (Monthly passenger traffic, 2004–2019). Includes tooltips, annotation, and dropdown/slider filters.

Chart 1: Monthly passengers by route (filterable)

Select the origin and destination, drag the start-year slider. The dashed rule and label mark the peak passenger month.

Chart 2: Top-10 routes by year (total passengers)

Drag the year slider to view the busiest routes. Bars are ranked by total passenger volume in that year.
